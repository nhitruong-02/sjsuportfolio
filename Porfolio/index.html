Artist statement

Hi, my name is Nhi, and I am a Digital Media Art student at San José State University. I enjoy creating art, making things, and trying out new creative ideas. Art has always interested me because it allows me to use my imagination and create something of my own. Through my university projects, I hope to keep improving my skills, explore different ways of creating art, and find my own style as an artist. I am especially interested in illustration, character design, digital and traditional art. I enjoy looking at cute characters and colorful artwork. These things inspire me when I create my own artwork. I like how small details, colors, and expressions can make a character feel more alive. I also enjoy seeing how different designs can communicate ideas and create different feelings. These interests encourage me to keep exploring art and learning more about the creative process. During my studies, I have worked on projects using different materials and software, including Adobe Illustrator and After Effects. This gave me experience with digital design and helped me explore how images and visual elements can be arranged. I found it interesting to see my design go from a sketch to a digital file and then into a physical object. These experiences showed me that art can take many forms and that different tools can help bring an idea to life. Not every project has been easy for me. Sometimes, I have difficulty coming up with ideas, learning how to use a program, or making the final result look the way I imagined. When this happens, I need to take my time, try different approaches, and make changes along the way. Although these challenges can be frustrating, they have taught me to be more patient and keep practicing. I have learned that making mistakes is part of the creative process and that I can improve by learning from them. Each assignment gives me another opportunity to understand the tools I use and become more comfortable with creating my own work. Studying Digital Media Art has also given me the opportunity to explore creative tools beyond the types of art I was already interested in. I want to learn more about different digital art techniques and discover new ways to express my ideas. I believe that trying unfamiliar tools and methods can help me find new interests and improve my creativity. Even when a project is challenging or different from what I usually do, I can still learn something useful from the experience. In the future, I hope to continue developing my skills in illustration, design, and other areas of digital media. I know I still have a lot to learn, but I want to keep exploring new ideas and become more confident in my work. As I continue my studies at SJSU, I hope to develop my own artistic style and create work that reflects my interests, imagination, and personal growth.
